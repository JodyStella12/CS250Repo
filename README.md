## Reflection

### How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating user stories help with this?

I interpret user needs by understanding what the user wants the product to accomplish and turning those needs into clear requirements. User stories help by describing features from the user's perspective and giving the development team a clear understanding of what should be built and why it is valuable.

### How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?

I approach developing programs by breaking larger requirements into smaller, manageable tasks and improving the product through testing and feedback. In future development work, I would like to use Agile practices such as sprint planning, daily Scrum meetings, sprint reviews, retrospectives, and continuous feedback. These practices help teams adapt to changes and improve throughout development.

### What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?

Being a good team member in software development means communicating clearly, collaborating with others, being accountable for my work, and being willing to give and receive feedback. A strong team member also respects different perspectives and helps the team work toward a shared goal. My experience with Scrum has shown me that communication and collaboration are essential for a successful Agile team.
